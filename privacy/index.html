Privacy Policy.

This policy explains how the SOMNEVORA website handles information when you browse our website and follow links to our products or external services.

01 / OVERVIEW

About this policy

SOMNEVORA is a WordPress software brand. This Privacy Policy applies to the SOMNEVORA brand website.

The current website primarily provides information about SOMNEVORA and its WordPress products. It does not currently provide user accounts, online checkout, or a SOMNEVORA-hosted customer dashboard.

02 / INFORMATION

Information we collect

The current SOMNEVORA website does not intentionally request personal information through account registration, checkout, or contact forms.

Like most websites, technical information may still be processed by the services used to deliver the website. This can include information such as an IP address, browser type, device information, request information, and similar technical data.

03 / HOSTING

Website hosting

The SOMNEVORA website may be hosted through third-party infrastructure, including GitHub Pages. The hosting provider may process technical information necessary to deliver, secure, and operate the website.

The handling of information by a hosting provider is also subject to that provider's own policies and practices.

04 / EXTERNAL SERVICES

Third-party links

SOMNEVORA pages may link to external websites, including WordPress.org and other relevant product or developer resources.

Once you follow a third-party link, the destination website is responsible for its own privacy practices. SOMNEVORA does not control how independent third-party websites process information.

05 / COOKIES

Cookies and analytics

The current SOMNEVORA website does not intentionally set advertising cookies or operate a SOMNEVORA advertising tracking system.

If analytics, embedded services, contact forms, advertising tools, or other technologies that process additional information are added in the future, this policy should be updated to reflect those changes.

SOMNEVORA's website functionality may evolve as the product brand grows. This policy describes the website in its current form.

06 / CHANGES

Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the website, its services, or its information-handling practices change.

When material changes are made, the effective date displayed on this page may also be updated.

07 / CONTACT

Privacy questions

If a public SOMNEVORA contact channel is added to the website, privacy-related questions can be directed through that channel.

Until then, this page serves as the current description of the SOMNEVORA website's privacy practices.
